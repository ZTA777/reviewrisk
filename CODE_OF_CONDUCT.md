# Code of Conduct

We follow the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.

Our community is committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone. Contributions should focus on technical evidence and impact rather than assumptions about a contributor's identity, motives, or level of experience.

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing acceptable behavior. Serious or sensitive conduct concerns should be reported privately to the repository owner through the contact method listed on their GitHub profile.

The full Contributor Covenant 2.1 text is available at https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/ .
